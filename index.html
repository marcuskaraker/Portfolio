Marcus Karåker

Game Developer

I am a game developer based in Stockholm, Sweden. I've been making games for over a decade which has taken me on journeys around the world. I have a deep love for the uniqueness of this particular art form and find joy in exploring many of its components like programming, game design, art, music, world building and narrative. In 2026, I founded the game studio Yore & Yonder together with Filip Bergqvist when we revealed our debut game Northward, a co-op sailing adventure game. We also co-founded the game development collective The Dev Cabinet together with Sean Falk and David Stenfors.
